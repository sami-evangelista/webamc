You must upload a zip archive obtained with the webamc compile tool. All items (MCQs and questions) included in the archive will be inserted in your database.

All questions in the archive which are not attached to an MCQ will be considered as standalone. This means that they can appear in a question pack.

MCQs in the archive will be in exam mode, and thus will not be visible by other users. You will be able to create an exam in the menuexam.

An MCQ may also be switch to the review mode from the database page. You must then select the user group(s) that will be able to see the MCQ.
